## 🛠 Troubleshooting & FAQ

### 1. The Dashboard is showing "Link Error"
* **Check Connectivity:** Ensure your mobile device has an active internet connection (Cellular or Wi-Fi).
* **Server Status:** Our backend is hosted on secure Render clusters. If the app has been inactive, it may take 30 seconds for the telemetry link to "wake up" on the first request.

### 2. My Node 811 Sensor isn't updating
* **Verify Hardware:** Ensure the Node 811 unit is powered and the status LED is blinking green (indicating a successful handshake with the LevelOne API).
* **Distance Calibration:** Ensure the ultrasonic sensor is clear of debris or fuel splashes to maintain accurate gallon readings.

### 3. Login Issues
* **Credentials:** If you have forgotten your enterprise credentials, please contact your facility administrator or ScoreKings account manager.
* **Session Expiry:** For security, LevelOne uses JWT-based sessions. If you are redirected to the login screen, simply re-authenticate to continue monitoring.
