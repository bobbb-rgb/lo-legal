# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: March 31, 2026**

**ScoreKings LLC** ("we," "us," or "our") operates the **LevelOne** mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal and telemetry data when you use our Service.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

### Types of Data Collected
* **Personal Data:** While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you ("Personal Data"), including Email address and First/Last name.
* **Telemetry & Industrial Data:** We collect real-time data from authorized hardware (e.g., Node 811), including fuel levels (gallons), sensor status, and timestamps of inventory drops.
* **Usage Data:** We may collect information on how the Service is accessed and used (e.g., device type, IP address, and browser version).

## 2. Use of Data
ScoreKings LLC uses the collected data for various purposes:
* To provide and maintain the LevelOne Dashboard.
* To notify you about changes to your inventory levels.
* To provide customer support and hardware troubleshooting.
* To monitor the usage of the Service to prevent unauthorized access.

## 3. Transfer of Data
Your information, including Personal Data, may be transferred to — and maintained on — computers located outside of your state or country where the data protection laws may differ. Your consent to this Privacy Policy followed by your submission of such information represents your agreement to that transfer.

## 4. Disclosure of Data
We may disclose your Personal Data in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to:
* To comply with a legal obligation.
* To protect and defend the rights or property of ScoreKings LLC.
* To prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing in connection with the Service.

## 5. Security of Data
The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data.

## 6. Data Deletion Rights
In compliance with App Store and Play Store regulations, users have the right to request the deletion of their account and all associated telemetry data. 
* To request data removal, please contact us at **legal@scorekings.com**. 
* Requests will be processed within 30 days.
