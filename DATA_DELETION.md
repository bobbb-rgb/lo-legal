# Data Deletion Policy

**Last Updated: March 31, 2026**

In accordance with global privacy regulations and App Store guidelines, **LevelOne** (a ScoreKings LLC product) provides a clear pathway for users to request the permanent deletion of their account and associated telemetry data.

## 1. Data Categories Subject to Deletion
Upon a verified request, ScoreKings LLC will permanently remove:
* **Account Information:** Name, email address, and encrypted credentials.
* **Telemetry History:** All historical fuel level logs, audit timestamps, and Node 811 sensor data associated with the user's UUID.
* **Notification Preferences:** All saved alert thresholds and contact methods.

## 2. How to Request Deletion
Users may initiate a data deletion request through the following methods:

### Method A: In-App Request
1. Navigate to **Settings**.
2. Select **Data Deletion Policy**.
3. Follow the contact link to generate a pre-filled deletion request email.

### Method B: Email Request
Users may email **legal@scorekings.com** with the subject line **"DATA DELETION REQUEST"**. Please include the email address associated with the account.

## 3. Processing Timeline
* **Verification:** We will send a confirmation link to the registered email address to verify the request.
* **Completion:** Once verified, data is purged from our production databases within **30 days**. 
* **Backups:** Residual data may exist in secure encrypted backups for up to 60 days before being completely overwritten.

## 4. Impact of Deletion
Please note that once an account is deleted, it cannot be recovered. Access to LevelOne real-time monitoring and historical audit logs for your hardware will be permanently revoked.
